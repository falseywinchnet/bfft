\section{The Decimation-in-Time Dual}
\label{sec:dit}

The Bruun remainder cascade descends from a time polynomial to spectral
residues and is naturally a decimation-in-frequency (DIF) walk.  An explicit
decimation-in-time (DIT) dual reverses the dependency order: bit-reversed time
atoms are merged into successively longer local spectra until the ordinary DFT
appears in natural order.

Let $\operatorname{rev}_L$ reverse $L$ binary digits and initialize
\begin{equation}
 Y_0[j]=x_{\operatorname{rev}_L(j)},\qquad0\le j<N.
 \label{eq:dit-seed}
\end{equation}
At rank $s$, put $e=2^s$.  For every block origin
$b\equiv0\pmod{2e}$ and $0\le j<e$, define
\begin{align}
 U&=Y_s[b+j],\qquad V=Y_s[b+j+e],\\
 W&=e^{-2\pi i j/(2e)}V,\nonumber\\
 Y_{s+1}[b+j]&=U+W,\\
 Y_{s+1}[b+j+e]&=U-W.
 \label{eq:dit-rank}
\end{align}

\begin{theorem}[Constructive DIT identity]
After rank $s$, each consecutive block of $2^{s+1}$ entries contains the
natural-order DFT of the corresponding bit-reversed time comb.  In particular,
\begin{equation}
 Y_L[k]=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x_ne^{-2\pi ikn/N}=X_k.
 \label{eq:dit-terminal}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $s=0$, \eqref{eq:dit-rank} forms the length-two DFTs of adjacent seeds.
Assume the claim for length $e$.  The even and odd local spectra of a length
$2e$ comb are the two child spectra $U$ and $V$; the shift between their time
supports contributes $e^{-2\pi ij/(2e)}$.  Equation \eqref{eq:dit-rank} is
therefore the exact length-$2e$ Fourier merge.  Induction reaches the complete
time set, and the initial bit reversal places its bits in the required merge
order.
\end{proof}

For real input, each local spectrum has conjugate symmetry.  Store a conjugate
pair as $(a,b)=(\Re X,-\Im X)$.  Multiplication by the unit phase in
\eqref{eq:dit-rank} becomes the real rotation $R_\theta$, and the two outputs
become the normalized cell \eqref{eq:normalized-cell}; DC and local Nyquist
coordinates use $H_2$.  The real folded DIT is consequently a schedule of the
same exact pair merges used by the normalized Bruun map.

\begin{corollary}[DIT inverse and metric]
Reversing the ranks, applying $T_\theta^{\trans}/2$ and $H_2^{\trans}/2$ to
their local coordinates, and undoing \eqref{eq:dit-seed} gives the two-sided
inverse.  After the terminal $\sqrt2$ pair packing, the real DIT boundary map is
$\DFT_N^{\R}$ and has Gram matrix $NI$.
\end{corollary}

More generally, if intermediate layouts are $J_s$, the conjugated ranks
$\widetilde S_s=J_{s+1}S_sJ_s^{-1}$ telescope to
$J_L(S_{L-1}\cdots S_0)J_0^{-1}$.  This identity explains why an internal sort
changes only the declared boundary layouts; equations
\eqref{eq:dit-seed}--\eqref{eq:dit-rank} supply the specific DIT construction.
