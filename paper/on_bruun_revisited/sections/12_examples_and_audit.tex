\section{Exact Examples and Finite-Precision Audit}
\label{sec:audit}

The examples first display exact identities.  The numerical tables then audit
binary64 evaluations of the already defined operators.

\subsection{The exact $N=4$ transform}

With the packing of \eqref{eq:packed-dft},
\begin{equation}
 \DFT_4^{\R}=
 \begin{bmatrix}
 1&1&1&1\\
 \sqrt2&0&-\sqrt2&0\\
 0&\sqrt2&0&-\sqrt2\\
 1&-1&1&-1
 \end{bmatrix}.
 \label{eq:dft4-matrix}
\end{equation}
Direct multiplication gives
$(\DFT_4^{\R})^{\trans}\DFT_4^{\R}=4I$.  Its middle two rows are $\sqrt2$
times the normalized residue coordinates of $x(z)$ modulo $z^2+1$; its first and last rows are
evaluation modulo $z-1$ and $z+1$.

For $N=8$, Fig.~\ref{fig:n8-tree} adds the two quadratic factors with
coefficients $\pm\sqrt2$.  Their normalized angles are $\pi/4$ and $3\pi/4$.
Every irrational coefficient is an element of $\K_8=\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$, so the
factorization and the CRT return are exact over that field.

An explicit radical form makes the leaf values visible.  Put
$d_j=x_j+x_{j+4}$, $p_j=x_j-x_{j+4}$ for $0\le j<4$, and $a=1/\sqrt2$.  Then
\begin{align}
 X_0&=d_0+d_1+d_2+d_3,\\
 X_4&=d_0-d_1+d_2-d_3,\\
 X_2&=(d_0-d_2)-i(d_1-d_3),\\
 X_1&=p_0+a(p_1-p_3)-i\,[p_2+a(p_1+p_3)],\\
 X_3&=p_0-a(p_1-p_3)-i\,[a(p_1+p_3)-p_2].
 \label{eq:n8-radical-spectrum}
\end{align}
The remaining bins are the conjugates of $X_1,X_2,X_3$.  Substitution into
\eqref{eq:dft-definition} proves these identities term by term, and the local
inverse cells recover each $d_j,p_j$ and then each $x_j$.

\subsection{Fourier and diagonal audits}

Table~\ref{tab:dip-audit} reports maximum absolute coordinate error against
direct Fourier summation for the real diagonal walk.  Inputs were successive
standard-normal vectors from NumPy PCG64 with seed 7, sampled in ascending
order of $N$.  The ordered and sheared complex Zak products agreed entrywise in
the binary64 evaluation, while their error against direct summation grew with
the ordinary accumulation length.

Let $\widehat B_N$ denote the binary64 evaluation of the exact normalized
factorization $B_N$ for the stated deterministic input.

The faithful monomial-coordinate CRT reduction at $N=512$ gave a forward residual
$7.0\times10^{-13}$ and a forward-inverse residual $1.1\times10^{-14}$.
For the normalized map, direct matrix audits over
$N=4,8,\ldots,512$ placed orthogonality residuals between $10^{-16}$ and
$7.5\times10^{-15}$, with measured condition number at most
$1.000000000000001$.  These measurements distinguish the algebraic identity
from its floating evaluation while confirming the normalization law.

\subsection{Positive-cone and equilibrium audit}

For a 512-point normalized factorization and a standard-normal PCG64 seed 43
input, the backward gauge produced an input
potential range $[256,1324]$.  The maximum column-stochasticity defect was
$2.22\times10^{-16}$, the projected forward residual was
$2.16\times10^{-14}$, and the forward-inverse residual was
$1.78\times10^{-15}$.  A normally distributed signed input permitted $97.4\%$
of the lifted mass to be removed as equal-rail common mode; an impulse required
no annihilation.

An exact-structure $N=8$ mechanical certificate is summarized in
Table~\ref{tab:mechanical-audit}.  The binary interpolation decomposition contained
44 bars---36 midpoint and eight with ratio $\sqrt2-1$---plus twelve
pass-through rows.  These counts describe one decomposition of the stochastic
stages, not additional assumptions in the realizability theorem.
