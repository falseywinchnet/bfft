# Causal predictive simmer: what survives the parity failure

## Why this experiment exists

The four-direction image lift showed that distributed relations survive while
sparse interfaces lose authority. The proposed cure was not another edge rule,
but exact causal ancestry carried by the V3 continuous-eikonal parent simplex.
This simmer implements that proposal far enough to separate the valid geometry
from an invalid source population.

Three artifacts contain the M4 evidence:

- `causal_parity_geometry.json`: direct ancestry population and value readout;
- `causal_predictive_geometry.json`: ordinary and horizontal Wasserstein
  population with 16/32-quantile refinement;
- `causal_fixed_point.json`: self-consistency descent through 16 remarches.

None of these forms is exposed in the GUI.

## Determinant-one relation metric

The complete-scale characteristic conductance is first retained separately for
the four provisional tangent families. With unit tangent \(\hat d\),

\[
C(x)=\frac{\sum_d g_d(x)\hat d\hat d^T}{\sum_d g_d(x)},
\qquad
M(x)=\sqrt{\det C(x)}\,C(x)^{-1}.
\]

Thus predictable directions are cheap, \(\det M=1\), and no metric-strength
setting exists. Constant observations produce the identity exactly. The four
directions remain a crystalline quadrature debt, not a promoted catalogue.

## Exact shared-label ancestry

Every root is given the same transport label so V3 may form a simplex between
fronts from different roots. Root identity remains a separate coordinate. For
the accepted parent simplex \((p,q,t)\),

\[
A_x=(1-t)A_p+tA_q,
\qquad
N_2(x)=\frac{1}{\sum_i A_x(i)^2}.
\]

The complete ancestry vector makes overlap exact. If two parent paths already
share a root, it is counted once; conductance magnitude cannot impersonate an
independent sample.

The first emission used the two checkerboard parity lanes. Each target value
was reconstructed only from roots in the opposite lane. This is topological
observation exclusion, not a noise-dependent mask.

## Direct parity readout is rejected

On six structures crossed with additive, heavy replacement, and mixed
corruption at 48 square, the direct root-value barycenter scored:

| Readout | MSE | SSIM | variance | range | edge |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| shared-label parity | 0.01979 | 0.337 | 1.098 | 1.033 | 0.383 |
| four-direction seed | 0.00736 | 0.608 | 0.695 | 0.846 | 0.436 |
| integrated FMMT | **0.00683** | **0.691** | 0.737 | 0.853 | **0.515** |

The readout does not collapse to the mean; it does the opposite and retains
corruption. Its ancestry population is almost the same two-parent event
everywhere: mean `1.877`, 90th percentile `1.999`. Corrupt and clean population
fields correlate only `0.199`, and edge/flat population is `1.063`. Exact
ancestry is not enough when source emission saturates the domain uniformly.

## The transported predictive law is retained

The root barycenter is not the state. The complete atomic law

\[
\mu_x=\sum_i A_x(i)\,\delta_{y_i}
\]

is resolved on a common quantile coordinate and then measured. Between 16 and
32 quantiles, ordinary Wasserstein population changes by only `0.300%` on
average (`1.263%` worst). The representation has a stable refinement limit.

Ordinary spatial Wasserstein differentiation still mistakes a common signal
translation for new population. In the scalar fibre the first exact horizontal
connection is the translation quotient

\[
m_x=\int_0^1 q_x(u)\,du,
\qquad
q_x^H(u)=q_x(u)-m_x,
\qquad
D_aq=\partial_a q-\partial_a m.
\]

Every translated law—including an affine signal with stationary residual
shape—then has one support unit. Distributional shape and spread changes remain.

Across the diverse M4 probe, the horizontal quotient lowered mean corrupt/clean
population inflation from `2.020x` to `1.720x` and the worst case from `3.501x`
to `2.433x`. By corruption family at 32 quantiles:

| Corruption | ordinary ratio | horizontal ratio |
|---|---:|---:|
| uniform additive | 1.241 | **1.137** |
| replacement 0.25 | 2.222 | **1.935** |
| mixed 0.25 | 2.597 | **2.088** |

This is the first positive 2-D theoretical evidence after the raw predictive
law's `13.96x` hair-population explosion. It is not yet sufficient under heavy
replacement, but both causal transport and horizontalization survive
falsification.

## Fixed-point descent exposes the remaining jaggedness

For marching metric \(M\) and horizontal geometry requested by its transported
law \(H[\mu(M)]\), the fused residual is

\[
\mathcal R(M)=
\frac{\lVert M-H[\mu(M)]\rVert_F^2}{\lVert M\rVert_F^2}.
\]

A determinant-renormalized line search accepts a remarch only when
\(\mathcal R\) decreases. There is no denoising time or chosen blend: step
halving is numerical descent and terminates at floating-point resolution.

On clean and mixed versions of all six structures, 16 continuations reduced
mean residual to `0.102` of its initial value. Nevertheless, zero of twelve
cases reached numerical equilibrium; two exhausted the descent direction and
ten reached the continuation guard. Maximum final residual remained `0.0602`.

More iterations are not the theoretical repair. Dense parity roots cause
discrete parent-simplex switches, and the fixed-point map settles onto a
nonsmooth residual shelf. That is exactly the jaggedness the fused system is
supposed to remove.
