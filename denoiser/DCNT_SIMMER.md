# DCNT: target-excluded resize transport

## Status

DCNT is the new foundational denoising experiment. It does not extend FMMT or
the lifted endpoint estimator. The first implementation is a falsifiable
Python/native hybrid, not a production denoiser and not ready for C++.

## 1. The TV control and the retained descent

`tv_chambolle_reference` is a literal reconstruction of scikit-image's
Chambolle recurrence, including its dual field, forward-difference boundary
closure, energy normalization, and stopping test. The local comparison test
matches scikit output bit-for-bit. DCNT does not use this solver.

The actual proximal descent is the repository's optimized Meyer ROF solve

\[
  \operatorname{ROF}_c(g)
  =\arg\min_u TV(u)+\frac c2\lVert u-g\rVert_2^2,
\]

run by Split Bregman with the objective-checked Fourier/Hodge closure. For a
field of RMS amplitude \(s\), homogeneity gives the dimensionless fidelity
\(cs=1\). Thus \(c=1/s\); the method does not borrow scikit's denoising
weight.

## 2. The four CONV observers

For every parity \(a=(a_y,a_x)\in\{0,1\}^2\), take the coarse lattice

\[
  Y_a(i,j)=y(a_y+2i,a_x+2j)
\]

and refine it with CONV*. Whole-sample reflection closes the compact stencil
without duplicating a boundary target. At a target pixel, discard the chart
for which that pixel was an input anchor. Exactly three predictions remain:

\[
  \mathcal P(p)=\{P_a(p):p\notin Y_a\},\qquad |\mathcal P(p)|=3.
\]

Changing only \(y(p)\) leaves all three values unchanged. This is an enforced
test invariant. Consequently the target cannot manufacture the support that
certifies itself.

Let \(m\) be their median and \(g_a=P_a-m\). Two transport sets are retained:

\[
  H_{\rm hull}=\max_a|g_a|,
  \qquad
  H_Z=\sum_a|g_a|.
\]

The first is the observed convex hull. The second is the one-dimensional
zonotope generated by uncertainty about all three transports. No distribution
class is named.

## 3. Minimal noise under uncertainty

For interval \(Z(p)=[m(p)-H(p),m(p)+H(p)]\), the unique minimal noise required
to make the observation compatible is

\[
  n_{\min}(p)=y(p)-\Pi_{Z(p)}y(p).
\]

The uncertainty branch applies Meyer descent to this *resistant* component.
Its ROF part is coherent unsupported structure and is returned to the scene;
only the complement is rejected:

\[
  n_{\rm coherent}=\operatorname{ROF}_{1/\lVert n_{\min}\rVert_{\rm RMS}}
       (n_{\min}),
\]

\[
  x=y-\bigl(n_{\min}-n_{\rm coherent}\bigr),
  \qquad r=y-x.
\]

Then the four observer charts are rebuilt on \(x\). At every cycle
\(x+r=y\) pointwise.

The companion `transport_descent` branch implements the first literal idea:
smooth the part inside the convex transport hull, reintegrate the resisting
part, and rebuild the resize observers. It is retained because the first
battery rejects it as the general readout.

## 4. First measured gate

The checked-in `dcnt_first_battery.json` contains three structurally different
32-square sources and five conditions: clean, Gaussian, uniform, replacement,
and mixed replacement/uniform. Values below average all 15 cases.

| method | MSE | SSIM | edge retention | seconds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| observation | .01647 | .5375 | .911 | ~0 |
| reconstructed TV | .00705 | .5591 | .420 | .0006 |
| DCNT transport descent | .01273 | .5200 | .612 | .158 |
| **DCNT uncertainty** | **.00670** | **.6311** | **.726** | .130 |
| integrated FMMT control | .00409 | .6941 | .539 | .024 |

The uncertainty branch is the positive result. It beats the reconstructed TV
control in aggregate MSE, SSIM, and especially retained edge energy. On mixed
damage it scores `.01225/.4315/.608`, versus TV's `.01175/.3733/.352`: nearly
the same MSE, more SSIM, and far more scene structure. It also preserves clean
woven chirps at MSE `.000141` and SSIM `.9925`, where TV gives `.005168` and
`.3361`.

FMMT remains the stronger cleanup engine by MSE and SSIM, particularly for
replacement damage, but its aggregate edge retention is `.539` versus DCNT's
`.726`. The new coordinate therefore carries information FMMT destroys. It is
not yet the final estimator.

The repeated 64-square gate in `dcnt_battery_64.json` preserves the finding
with a more honest image scale. DCNT uncertainty records
`.00632/.5907/.759` against TV's `.00607/.5495/.420`: TV is 4.1% lower in
MSE, while DCNT has higher SSIM and 81% more retained edge response. FMMT is
still cleaner at `.00289/.7209/.595`. Thus the result is a structure/error
trade, not a claim that DCNT already dominates either control.

## 5. What failed and what comes next

Smoothing the transport-admitted component is not sufficient. Noise that
resists resize is simply reinserted, and the branch loses both MSE and SSIM.
That is a useful rejection of the most literal first optional step.

The zonotope still moves clean Cameraman and geometric interfaces, and three
cycles are a numerical experiment rather than an intrinsic stopping theorem.
The next state should retain the separate CONV generators through time and
transport their covariance/phase, instead of collapsing each cycle to one
interval. That supplies the missing distinction between coherent fine detail
and an incoherent target outlier while keeping uncertainty about transport
itself explicit.
