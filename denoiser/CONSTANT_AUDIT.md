# Constant and testing audit of the supplied FMMT checkpoint

This audit treats constants as suspect when they choose physical support,
authority, propagation, stopping, or posterior trust. It does not pretend that
all numbers are arbitrary: identities such as \(\sqrt2\), probability
normalizations, graph connectivity, array dimensions, and CFL safety factors
are mathematical or numerical structure.

The replacement target is now stricter than converting these constants one at
a time. `V3_FUSED_DENOISING_THEORY.md` derives support population from the
horizontal Wasserstein volume of one transported joint signal/residual/jet
measure. Its determinant-normalized tensor supplies the eikonal metric, so
metric strength, support thresholds, and travel bands have no corresponding
runtime parameter. The scalar empirical pullback uses exact quantile coupling:
there are no histogram bins or bandwidths. The tested immediate relation
catalogue is retained only as a rejected seed, not promoted as a new stencil.

## Testing that must not become the law

The supplied benchmark legitimately evaluates with clean truth, but several
branches were selected from finite screens:

- support scales 8 and 12;
- the 0.14/0.22 censor transition;
- the 16/32 residual-tail transition;
- 128 support sweeps;
- the decision to retain the graph-barrier gate only after an ablation win;
- benchmark baselines selected by clean-truth oracle over median window, TV
  weight, or Wiener window.

Those tests are valuable falsification evidence. They must remain outside the
runtime state equation. The new probe similarly does **not** tune its law to
maximize its reported edge score; every failed intermediate is recorded in
`ANALYTIC_UPLIFT.md`.

## Structural constants retained without apology

- two parity lanes are the minimum disjoint cross-observation partition;
- pairwise flux uses equal and opposite updates because conservation demands it;
- coordinate faces in a finite-volume grid arise from topology, not from an
  image-content direction catalogue;
- \(2\pi\) converts the shortest domain period into a Gaussian scale;
- the Courant fraction is a numerical stability margin and is reported as
  resolution metadata;
- machine epsilon and floating-point tiny values prevent undefined divisions;
- grayscale range `[0,1]` is the input coordinate convention, not a noise model.
- FMMT front-batch size and its memory cap change only dense workspace shape;
  batch-invariance is tested and the resolved value is reported.
