\section{Finite CONV* Banks for Joint Warped-Pixel Transfer}
\label{app:convstarwarp}

Let $Y:\Lambda\to\mathbb R^c$ be a finite Cartesian source field. CONV
assigns signed currents to source intervals, synthesizes two Cartesian factor
orders, and combines them by a scalar action coordinate. Joint current
admission then produces a continuous tensor-quintic control lattice $P(Y)$.
This section compiles that construction and its warped-pixel functionals into
finite banks. The coefficients carry the admitted source state, the
continuous synthesis phase, and the integration geometry.

\subsection{Finite global action banks}
Let $\eta_a\in[0,1]$ be the nodal order coordinate at source site $a$ and
$d_M(x,a)$ the distance of the source-conditioned positive-definite metric.
At an off-node query $x$, define
\begin{equation}
 h_M(x,a)=d_M(x,a)^{-2},\qquad
 \beta(x)=\frac{\sum_{a\in\Lambda}h_M(x,a)\eta_a}
 {\sum_{a\in\Lambda}h_M(x,a)}.
 \label{eq:starglobalbank}
\end{equation}
The value at a source node is assigned by cardinal extension,
$\beta(a)=\eta_a$. For a finite query set, the two sums are applications of
one finite matrix to the columns $\eta$ and $\mathbf1$. Positivity gives
$\min_a\eta_a\leq\beta(x)\leq\max_a\eta_a$.

\begin{proposition}[Global FIR realization]
Suppose $M$ is constant and source coordinates are in lattice units. Let
$m$ be the indicator of $\Lambda$, extended by zero, and let
$\phi\in[0,1)^2$ be an off-node phase. On the finite set of required source
and query differences, put
\begin{equation}
 k_\phi[r]=\bigl((r+\phi)^TM(r+\phi)\bigr)^{-1}.
\end{equation}
With $(k*f)[n]=\sum_a k[n-a]f[a]$, the global action satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \beta(n+\phi)=\frac{(k_\phi*(m\eta))[n]}{(k_\phi*m)[n]}.
 \label{eq:starglobalfir}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For a constant metric, $d_M(n+\phi,a)^2=(n-a+\phi)^TM(n-a+\phi)$.
Substitution into \eqref{eq:starglobalbank} gives the numerator and denominator
in \eqref{eq:starglobalfir}. Zero extension of $m$ includes precisely the
finite source domain. The node value follows from the dominant self term as
$x\to a$.
\end{proof}

Each phase therefore determines two finite convolutions with a shared kernel.
Zero padding to the full linear-convolution extent permits their Fourier
execution. For a spatially varying metric, the corresponding bank is
$h_M(n+\phi,a)$ with both indices retained. Metric construction and distance
evaluation establish this bank once for a fixed source and query geometry.
The bilinear nodal coordinate $\beta^*=I_h\eta$ supplies a separate compact
choice. For either coordinate the factor bank below has the same form, and
changing the coordinate gives the identity
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal T_{\beta^*}Y-\mathcal T_\beta Y
 =(\beta^*-\beta)(\mathcal T_{yx}Y-\mathcal T_{xy}Y).
\end{equation}

\subsection{Active-face synthesis and two-dimensional composition}
On a unit source interval, write $a_k=K_kY$ for the five raw currents,
$\delta=DY$ for the source difference, and $eY$ for the left endpoint.
The endpoint closures are included in $K_k,D,e$. The integrated Bernstein
tails are
\begin{equation}
 \tau_k(u)=\sum_{j=k+1}^{5}B_j^5(u),\qquad 0\leq k\leq4.
\end{equation}
Let $J$ be the support of the admitted current. On a nonempty active face,
the Euclidean signed-fibre projection gives
\begin{equation}
 c_k=\begin{cases}
 a_k+\displaystyle\frac{\delta-\sum_{l\in J}a_l}{|J|},&k\in J,\\
 0,&k\notin J.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:starfacecurrent}
\end{equation}
The ordered ledger and projection determine $J$ from the input line.

\begin{proposition}[Face-indexed FIR synthesis]
For $J\ne\varnothing$, define
\begin{align}
 \bar\tau_J(u)&=|J|^{-1}\sum_{k\in J}\tau_k(u),\nonumber\\
 W_J(u)&=e+\bar\tau_J(u)D\nonumber\\
 &\quad+\sum_{k\in J}(\tau_k(u)-\bar\tau_J(u))K_k.
 \label{eq:starfacefir}
\end{align}
Then the admitted factor value equals $W_J(u)Y$. For the empty face,
$W_\varnothing(u)=e$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Insert \eqref{eq:starfacecurrent} into $eY+\sum_k\tau_k(u)c_k$ and collect
the coefficients of $\delta$ and $a_k$. If $J$ is empty, all admitted
currents vanish and synthesis returns the anchor.
\end{proof}

There are 31 nonempty face families. Each is polynomial in $u$ of degree at
most five and has six-tap interior support. Lineage is retained in the choice
of family. The factor can consequently be evaluated by one stencil after
its face has been established.

For a source cell $(i,j)$ and phase $(u,v)$, let
$W^x_{i,j+s}(u;r)$ be the first-factor stencil on row $j+s$.
Let $W^y_{i,j,u}(v;s)$ be the second-factor stencil established from the line
of first-factor values at $i+u$.

\begin{theorem}[Conditioned two-dimensional factor bank]
The horizontal--vertical order has the finite representation
\begin{align}
 K^{xy}_{i,j,u,v}[r,s]
 &=W^y_{i,j,u}(v;s)W^x_{i,j+s}(u;r),\nonumber\\
 (\mathcal T_{xy}Y)(i+u,j+v)
 &=\sum_{r,s}K^{xy}_{i,j,u,v}[r,s]Y_{i+r,j+s}.
 \label{eq:startwodbank}
\end{align}
Constructing $K^{yx}$ with exchanged axes gives the complete blended bank
\begin{equation}
 K_\beta=(1-\beta)K^{xy}+\beta K^{yx}.
 \label{eq:starblendedbank}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \eqref{eq:starfacefir} first to each source row and then to the resulting
transverse line. Substitution of the first finite sum into the second gives
\eqref{eq:startwodbank}. The convex factor blend gives
\eqref{eq:starblendedbank}.
\end{proof}

The interior support is six by six. Different source rows may have different
first-factor faces, so the bank is generally nonseparable. On an interval of
$u$ where the second-factor face is fixed, its coefficients are independent
of $u$; the first-factor values remain quintic in $u$. Each order is then
bicquintic on the corresponding phase region. With bilinear $\beta^*$, the
blend has bidegree at most $(6,6)$ on a region where both orders have fixed
faces. Ledger-choice and active-face inequalities certify such regions; phase evaluation within
a certified region reuses the same polynomial coefficients.

For an endpoint-aligned rational lattice, write the reduced source-coordinate
step on each axis as $a_d/b_d$. The fractional phases belong to the $b_d$
residue classes modulo one. Their Cartesian product supplies at most
$b_xb_y$ phase pairs, with boundary closures and admitted face indices retained
as bank indices. Continuous query coordinates evaluate the same polynomial
families directly.

\subsection{Compilation through joint current admission}
The joint atlas uses a fixed fifth-step collocation map followed by shared
range and current admission. Let $B(Y)$ be the degree-elevated bilinear
baseline, $\widetilde P(Y)$ the collocation proposal, and $\widehat P(Y)$ its
range projection. Assign each nonvertex control to one shared entity $g$.
If $\alpha_g$ is its simultaneous capacity and $\tau$ the common completion
parameter, define
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_g=\alpha_g+\tau(1-\alpha_g),\qquad
 P_e=B_e+\lambda_g(\widehat P_e-B_e),\quad e\in g.
 \label{eq:starentityweight}
\end{equation}
Vertex controls retain their source values.

\begin{theorem}[Source-state factorization]
Let $S(Y)$ record the faces and order coordinates used at collocation nodes,
the selected range-bound source indices, and the admitted values of
$\lambda_g$. For each channel there is a finite matrix $A_{S(Y)}$ such that
\begin{equation}
 P(Y)=A_{S(Y)}Y.
 \label{eq:staratlasfactorization}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
With the recorded faces and coordinates, \eqref{eq:starblendedbank} makes each
collocation value a linear source stencil. Inverse Bernstein collocation and
bilinear degree elevation are fixed linear maps. Range projection selects
one of three linear values: the proposal, the selected lower-bound sample,
or the selected upper-bound sample. Equation~\eqref{eq:starentityweight}
then combines two linear stencils with recorded scalar coefficients.
Assembling the shared entities gives $A_{S(Y)}$.
\end{proof}

For fixed $Y$, this factorization retains the exact admitted controls and
therefore their cardinality, continuity, support-range bounds, and current
cones. State discovery consists of line scans, finite projections, support
extrema, incident-constraint minima, and the common ray reduction. Once that
state is available, a source stencil can be propagated through every linear
stage or the intermediate control lattice can be retained. Both evaluate
\eqref{eq:staratlasfactorization}.

\subsection{Point and area banks on the admitted atlas}
Use source-pixel coordinates on the source rectangle $\Omega$. Let
$F$ map target coordinates to source coordinates and let $G=F^{-1}$.
Assume the map is nonsingular and has no denominator zero on the domain of
integration. Put $Q=G(\Omega)$ and extend the source field by zero outside
$Q$ in target coordinates. On a source cell $C$, write
\begin{equation}
 U_C(u,v)=\sum_{i,j=0}^5P_{C,ij}B_i^5(u)B_j^5(v).
\end{equation}
Point synthesis uses the continuous phase bank
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_{u,v}[i,j]=B_i^5(u)B_j^5(v).
 \label{eq:starphasebank}
\end{equation}
For a target basin $E$ of positive area, let
$S_C=F(E\cap Q)\cap C$ and $J_G=|\det DG|$. Define the cell moment bank
\begin{equation}
 M_{C,ij}(E,G)=\int_{S_C}B_i^5(u)B_j^5(v)J_G(u,v)\,du\,dv.
 \label{eq:starmomentbank}
\end{equation}
A change of variables gives
\begin{equation}
 (\mathcal B_FY)_E=\frac1{|E|}\sum_{C,i,j}M_{C,ij}P_{C,ij}.
 \label{eq:starareabank}
\end{equation}
After assembly of shared controls and division by $|E|$, denote this bank
by $\mathsf M_E$. The complete source-to-output operators are
\begin{equation}
 (\mathcal W_FY)(q)=\Psi_{F(q)}^TA_{S(Y)}Y,\qquad
 (\mathcal B_FY)_E=\mathsf M_E^TA_{S(Y)}Y.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Positivity and conservation]
The exact moment weights are nonnegative and satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{C,i,j}M_{C,ij}=|E\cap Q|.
\end{equation}
For any basin partition $\{E_b\}$,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_b|E_b|(\mathcal B_FY)_{E_b}
 =\int_{(\cup_bE_b)\cap Q}U_Y(F(q))\,dq.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The Bernstein basis and $J_G$ are nonnegative. Summing the basis gives one
on each source cell. The cell footprints partition $F(E\cap Q)$ up to
boundaries of zero area, and change of variables gives $|E\cap Q|$.
Summation over the target partition proves the second identity.
\end{proof}

For numerical moment construction, a constant reference $R$ can be integrated
through target coverage:
\begin{align}
 (\mathcal B_FY)_E
 &=\frac{R|E\cap Q|}{|E|}\nonumber\\
 &\quad+\frac1{|E|}\sum_{C,i,j}(P_{C,ij}-R)M_{C,ij}.
 \label{eq:starwarpfunctional}
\end{align}
This identity holds for any $R$. A reference chosen by a fixed linear
functional of the controls preserves the assembled linear-bank form when
approximate moments are substituted.

\subsection{Exact affine moment banks}
For a rectangular footprint $[a,b]\times[c,d]$ in a cell and constant density
$\rho$, define
\begin{equation}
 A_i(t)=\frac16\sum_{j=i+1}^6B_j^6(t),\qquad 0\leq i\leq5.
\end{equation}
The Bernstein derivative identity telescopes to $A_i'=B_i^5$, yielding
\begin{equation}
 M_{C,ij}=\rho[A_i(b)-A_i(a)][A_j(d)-A_j(c)].
 \label{eq:starwarprectangle}
\end{equation}
For a general affine map the cell footprint is polygonal and the integrand
is polynomial of total degree at most ten. Its monomial moments can be
obtained by an oriented boundary sum:
\begin{equation}
 \int_D x^ay^b\,dx\,dy
 =\frac1{a+1}\oint_{\partial D}x^{a+1}y^b\,dy.
 \label{eq:starpolymoment}
\end{equation}
On each line segment, affine parameterization reduces the integrand to a
polynomial in one variable. Equations \eqref{eq:starwarprectangle} and
\eqref{eq:starpolymoment} thus determine finite exact affine banks directly
from footprint vertices.

\subsection{Exact projective moment banks}
Write $G$ in homogeneous coordinates with nonsingular matrix $H_G$ and affine
denominator $\ell(x,y)$. Its area density is
\begin{equation}
 J_G(x,y)=\frac{|\det H_G|}{|\ell(x,y)|^3}.
\end{equation}
Choose a nonzero scale $c_0$ of the same sign as $\ell$ on the footprint,
and write $\ell=c_0t$, where $t=\gamma+\alpha x+\beta y>0$.
Let $\rho=|\det H_G|/|c_0|^3$.

\begin{theorem}[Finite projective edge compilation]
Every entry of \eqref{eq:starmomentbank} on a polygonal cell footprint is a
finite expression in its vertices, the map coefficients, rational operations,
and logarithms of positive denominator values.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $\alpha\ne0$, transform to $(t,y)$ so that
$x=(t-\gamma-\beta y)/\alpha$ and $dx\,dy=|\alpha|^{-1}dt\,dy$.
The transformed polygon $D$ is given counterclockwise orientation. Expand
one tensor Bernstein numerator after substitution as
$\sum_{k,l}b_{kl}t^ky^l$, with $k+l\leq10$. Its bank entry is
\begin{equation}
 M=\frac{\rho}{|\alpha|}\sum_{k,l}b_{kl}I_{kl}(D),\qquad
 I_{kl}(D)=\int_D t^{k-3}y^l\,dt\,dy.
\end{equation}
Green's theorem gives
\begin{equation}
 I_{kl}(D)=-\frac1{l+1}\oint_{\partial D}t^{k-3}y^{l+1}\,dt.
 \label{eq:starprojectiveedge}
\end{equation}
For an oriented edge from $(t_0,y_0)$ to $(t_1,y_1)$ with $t_0\ne t_1$,
put $a=(y_1-y_0)/(t_1-t_0)$ and $b=y_0-at_0$. Its contribution is
\begin{equation}
 -\frac1{l+1}\sum_{j=0}^{l+1}\binom{l+1}{j}
 a^jb^{l+1-j}\mathcal L_{k-3+j}(t_0,t_1),
 \label{eq:staredgeprimitive}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L_e(a,b)=
 \begin{cases}
 (b^{e+1}-a^{e+1})/(e+1),&e\ne-1,\\
 \log(b/a),&e=-1.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
An edge with $t_0=t_1$ contributes zero. If $\alpha=0$ and $\beta\ne0$,
exchange the source axes. If both vanish, the density is constant and
\eqref{eq:starpolymoment} applies.
\end{proof}

The same edge moments serve all 36 basis numerators and every channel.
Boundary terms on oppositely oriented shared edges cancel when their chosen
Green primitives have the same edge trace.
An oriented edge table can therefore reuse primitives across neighboring
footprints while preserving each cell's polynomial coefficients.

For close positive $a,b$, set $h=(b-a)/a$. The primitive difference has the
continuous form
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L_e(a,b)=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\frac{a^{e+1}}{e+1}
 \operatorname{expm1}((e+1)\operatorname{log1p}(h)),&e\ne-1,\\
 \operatorname{log1p}(h),&e=-1.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
This evaluates the power and logarithm differences without subtracting their
nearly equal endpoint values. The factors $a^j b^{l+1-j}$ in
\eqref{eq:staredgeprimitive} still require control of cancellation for nearly
constant-denominator edges. Scaled cell coordinates, selection of the larger
of $|\alpha|,|\beta|$, and the geometry-selected positive quadrature in
Section~\ref{sec:stargeometric} provide evaluation across these
configurations and the affine limit. The finite edge formula supplies an
independent analytic reference for the geometric moment construction.

\subsection{Geometric compilation of projective banks}
\label{sec:stargeometric}
Triangulate each clipped cell footprint. The canonical numerical construction
uses a positive triangle rule selected by denominator geometry and evaluates
$J_G$ directly at its nodes. On a triangle $T$, let
$[\ell_-,\ell_+]$ be the same-sign denominator range and put
\begin{equation}
 c_T=\frac{\ell_-+\ell_+}{2},\qquad
 z=\frac{\ell}{c_T}-1,\qquad
 r_T=\frac{\ell_+-\ell_-}{2|c_T|}<1.
 \label{eq:stargeometricradius}
\end{equation}
The extrema occur at the three vertices. With
$\rho_T=|\det H_G|/|c_T|^3$, the density is
$J_G=\rho_T(1+z)^{-3}$. The radius $r_T$ is unchanged by nonzero rescaling
of the homogeneous matrix.

Let $\mathcal Q_K$ be a positive-weight triangle rule exact through total
degree $10+K$, with physical weights $w_l>0$ satisfying
$\sum_lw_l=|T|$. Define
\begin{align}
 \widehat M_{T,ij}
 &=\sum_{l=1}^{L_K}w_lJ_G(p_l)
 B_i^5(p_{l,x})B_j^5(p_{l,y}),\nonumber\\
 \widehat I_{T,a}
 &=\sum_{i,j}(P_{C,ij,a}-R_a)\widehat M_{T,ij}.
 \label{eq:starintegratedpolyphase}
\end{align}
Every channel uses the same 36 nonnegative moment weights. Forming these
weights before channel contraction gives $36L_K$ geometric contributions
and $36c$ control contributions per triangle.

For barycentric coordinates $(1-u-v,u,v)$ on a triangle with vertices
$a,b,d$, the node denominator is
\begin{equation}
 \ell(p)=\ell(a)+u[\ell(b)-\ell(a)]+v[\ell(d)-\ell(a)].
 \label{eq:stardenominatorreuse}
\end{equation}
The three values used to select the rule therefore also evaluate its density.
When $r_T=0$, one constant density serves all nodes.

Choose the first row containing $r_T$. If no row contains it, join the three

The source controls and channel values do not enter this choice. The

\begin{proposition}[Finite geometric partition]
For a fixed nonsingular homography whose denominator has no zero on the
footprint, midpoint subdivision terminates after finitely many levels.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Compactness gives $m_\ell=\min|\ell|>0$. On a triangle of diameter $h$,
$\ell_+-\ell_-\leq\|\nabla\ell\|h$ and $|c_T|\geq m_\ell$.
Each midpoint child has half its parent's diameter. After $n$ levels,
\begin{equation}
 r_T\leq\frac{\|\nabla\ell\|h_0}{2^{n+1}m_\ell}.
\end{equation}
This eventually lies below the final table radius. Constant denominators
use the first row directly.
\end{proof}

\subsubsection{Uniform accuracy of the moment operator}
The table is established from polynomial exactness and a uniform remainder.
Write
\begin{equation}
 S_K(z)=\sum_{k=0}^K(-1)^k\binom{k+2}{2}z^k.
\end{equation}
For $n\geq1$, the absolute series tail is
\begin{align}
 T_n(r)&=\sum_{k=n}^\infty\binom{k+2}{2}r^k\nonumber\\
 &=\frac{r^n}{2}\left[
 \frac{r(1+r)}{(1-r)^3}+\frac{(2n+3)r}{(1-r)^2}\right.\nonumber\\
 &\hspace{20mm}\left.+\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}{1-r}\right].
 \label{eq:starwarptail}
\end{align}
The closed form follows by writing $k=n+j$ and summing $1,j,j^2$ against
$r^j$. Hence $|(1+z)^{-3}-S_K(z)|\leq T_{K+1}(r_T)$.

\begin{theorem}[Geometry-only moment accuracy]
Let $m_T=\int_TJ_G$. A positive rule exact through degree $10+K$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \frac{\sum_{i,j}|\widehat M_{T,ij}-M_{T,ij}|}{m_T}
 \leq 2(1+r_T)^3T_{K+1}(r_T).
 \label{eq:stargeometricerror}
\end{equation}
Every row of the geometric table makes the right-hand side at most
$\varepsilon_g=10^{-9}$. The same mass-relative bound holds after assembling
all triangle pieces of a footprint.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $b_{ij}=B_i^5B_j^5$. Its total degree is at most ten, so
$\mathcal Q_K$ integrates $b_{ij}S_K(z)$ exactly. Only the remainder
$R_K=(1+z)^{-3}-S_K(z)$ contributes to the moment defect. Positivity of the
rule and $\sum_{i,j}b_{ij}=1$ give
\begin{align}
 \sum_{i,j}|\widehat M_{T,ij}-M_{T,ij}|
 &\leq\rho_T\left(\int_T|R_K|+
 \mathcal Q_K(|R_K|)\right)\nonumber\\
 &\leq2|T|\rho_TT_{K+1}(r_T).
\end{align}
Since $m_T\geq|T|\rho_T(1+r_T)^{-3}$,
\eqref{eq:stargeometricerror} follows. The right-hand side is nondecreasing
in $r_T$; substitution of each table endpoint gives the stated budget.
The triangle inequality and additivity of Jacobian mass preserve it under
assembly.
\end{proof}

The polynomial $S_K$ supplies the proof of the fixed table. The numerical
bank in \eqref{eq:starintegratedpolyphase} evaluates the full rational
density. Runtime planning consists of the vertex range, table lookup and
geometric subdivision.

\begin{proposition}[Pixel-functional accuracy]
For a target basin $E$, let
$D_{E,a}=\max_{C,i,j}|P_{C,ij,a}-R_a|$ over its contributing cells.
Substitution of the geometric moments into \eqref{eq:starwarpfunctional}
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 |(\widehat{\mathcal B}_FY)_{E,a}-(\mathcal B_FY)_{E,a}|
 \leq\varepsilon_gD_{E,a}\frac{|E\cap Q|}{|E|}.
 \label{eq:starwarperror}
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Contract the assembled absolute moment defect with the largest residual
control and use $\sum_Tm_T=|E\cap Q|$. Division by $|E|$ gives the result.
\end{proof}

A constant field is reproduced through exact target coverage by choosing
its constant value as $R$. For a partition of target basins, the absolute
error in the area-weighted total is at most
$\varepsilon_g\sum_E D_{E,a}|E\cap Q|$. A reference obtained from a fixed
linear evaluation of the controls retains the source-state bank
factorization. The geometric partition and its moments can be reused for
any controls on the same lattice and warp.

\subsubsection{Verification of the fixed construction}
The degree-ten through degree-sixteen rules reproduce all reference-triangle
monomials through their declared degrees with maximum relative discrepancy
below $3\times10^{-14}$ in the retained tables. Their nodes lie in the
triangle and their weights are positive. Independent finite edge evaluation
at 220 decimal digits checks all 36 computed moments for 57 triangle and
denominator configurations. These include both sides of every positive
radius threshold, skew and thin triangles, and denominator ratios through
$1999:1$. The largest measured mass-relative sum of absolute weight errors
is $8.70\times10^{-10}$, including the floating-point subdivision and
accumulation in the tested implementation.

A separate pixel-functional check uses six source families, six homographies
and three output sizes. The families include smooth oscillations, alternating
samples, noise, a constant field, an isolated impulse and nearly transparent
samples. Against a reference evaluated with a $10^{-12}$ pixel tolerance,
8,640 sampled comparisons have maximum premultiplied-channel difference
$1.33\times10^{-13}$. All source families produce the same geometric rule
and subdivision counts for a common map and lattice. The retained moment
and pixel receipts accompany the direct-warp geometric compilation study.

\subsection{Composition, reuse, and numerical enclosures}
The complete dataflow is
\begin{equation}
 Y\longmapsto S(Y)\longmapsto A_{S(Y)}Y
 \longmapsto\{\Psi_q^TP,\mathsf M_E^TP\}.
\end{equation}
Independent lines within each factor share one raw-current bank. The next
factor consumes their synthesized values. Shared-entity capacities are
minima over incident constraints; the completion parameter is a global
minimum. These associative reductions admit parallel trees, while exact
lineage uses a prefix scan. Once $P$ is fixed, target basins and channel
contractions can be partitioned independently. Repeated phase pairs reuse
synthesis weights; repeated footprints reuse moment banks; common polygon
edges reuse oriented primitives. For $N_T$ triangle pieces, moment-first
quadrature has contraction work proportional to
$\sum_{T=1}^{N_T}(36L_T+36c)$, in addition to geometry construction.

The identities above hold in real arithmetic. A numerical enclosure can be
assembled from storage, moment, and contraction errors. If stored controls
satisfy $|\widehat P_e-P_e|\leq\delta_e$ and computed unnormalized exact-bank
approximations satisfy $|\widehat M_e-M_e|\leq\mu_e$, then
\begin{align}
 \left|\sum_e\widehat M_e\widehat P_e-\sum_eM_eP_e\right|
 &\leq\sum_e|\widehat M_e|\delta_e\nonumber\\
 &\quad+\sum_e\mu_e|P_e|.
\end{align}
The final summation contributes its arithmetic enclosure and the result is
divided by $|E|$. For reference-subtracted geometric banks,
\eqref{eq:starwarperror} supplies the moment-approximation contribution.
Bounds on computed coverage, primitive evaluation, and summation are added
