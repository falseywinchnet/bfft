# V3-native object transport research plan

## Established starting point

V3 regions are the immutable spatial atoms. The fused Meyer decomposition and
the wavelet-leader charts are measurements on those atoms; neither may alter
the segmentation. Role, contour, enclosure, amodal depth, proposal assembly,
raw leader content, and leader scale law remain typed coordinates.

The current evidence rules out three shortcuts:

1. dense similarity is not object transport;
2. exact pixel overlap across independently inferred resolutions is location
   alignment, not structural inheritance;
3. diffusing content similarity—directly or after boundary-role gating—joins
   repeated material and can make shuffled controls stronger.

The useful signature is instead `H_structural K_content H_structural`: content
is payload carried by sparse measured structure. It is promising at selected
scales but is not yet resolution-stable, so no current operator is promoted as
the object decomposer.

## Next mathematical object: soft object packets

The next implementation will not begin by forcing a partition. For every V3
region `q`, it will emit the complete normalized kernel column `K_object[:,q]`
as a soft object packet and recompose the source with that column as continuous
alpha. This exposes the full field without a threshold, seed chosen by the
algorithm, expected object count, or iterative clustering rule. Evaluation
anchors select columns only after inference.

The first scalar `K_object` control was constructed from independently
auditable coordinates:

- the existing complete role/contour/enclosure part kernel;
- raw wavelet-leader content transported as payload by proposal half-heat;
- leader scale-law content transported separately by the same half-heat;
- ordered incidence endpoint evidence, retained as a distinct coordinate
  because its astronaut gain survives the shuffle at every resolution.

The coordinates entered the complete positive Schur algebra with unit
algebraic multiplicity. Raw leader and scale law were not concatenated,
averaged, or selected by image. Content never became a dense transition
generator. This scalar collapse failed coffee even though its raw payload
coordinate succeeded, so the next packet must remain vector-valued across
typed coordinates through evaluation and recomposition.

The first extraction audit is projective rather than classificatory. It will
measure symmetry, positivity, packet self-return, packet overlap, and the full
spectral measure of `K_object`. Every spectral projector is retained initially;
no eigengap or component count is selected. A discrete object quotient is a
later promotion and must be justified by exact or resolution-persistent
idempotents, not imposed in advance.

## Image and recomposition ladder

1. **Pikachu easy.** Establish the ordinary disconnected-part relation and
   render body-, left-tip-, right-tip-, and surround-anchored soft packets.
2. **Pikachu hard.** Use the frozen black exterior and eight-pixel white wall.
   This is the primary transport control: both black tips must prefer the
   yellow body to the black surround. The wall and surround must remain
   separate packets.
3. **Coffee.** Recompose cup wall, liquid, plate, spoon, and table anchors.
   Cup/liquid/plate may assemble; spoon and table must not inherit that packet.
4. **Astronaut.** Recompose flag-blue, flag-red/white, suit, face, helmet, and
   rocket anchors. Flag fragments must unite without recruiting the suit or
   rocket. This is the main ordered-endpoint control.
5. **Coins and checker.** These are anti-shortcut controls. Coins may share a
   material coordinate but must remain separable instances; checker squares
   may share role while the board remains a different level of organization.
6. **Diagnostic perturbations.** Rerun the exact pipeline at 128/256/384,
   under RGB complement, and on a frozen wall-gap series. These variants probe
   transport support and sampling; they never tune the canonical operator.

Every atlas will show source, V3 atoms, each typed coordinate, the complete
packet, its shuffled null, and the continuously alpha-recomposed source.

## Matched nulls and promotion gates

Every proposed coordinate must be compared with a topology-preserving null:

- shuffle outside-state correspondence for directed incidence;
- shuffle bounded-manifold membership for proposal assembly;
- shuffle leader-to-region correspondence for content;
- shuffle cross-resolution alignment when a scale carrier is tested;
- exact RGB complement for contrast equivariance.

Promotion requires all of the following without changing the operator:

- both hard Pikachu tip/body inequalities at 128, 256, and 384;
- cup/plate above cup/spoon and cup/table, plus liquid/plate above
  plate/spoon, at exact points and the frozen aperture audit;
- every sampled flag/flag relation above its matched flag/suit and
  flag/rocket relations;
- a positive real-minus-shuffle margin on the same named relations;
- no collapse of all coins into one object packet and no replacement of the
  checker role field by generic content similarity;
- deterministic output and exact permutation covariance of region indexing.

Aggregate AUC remains a compact screen, not a promotion criterion. Named
relations, full fields, recompositions, nulls, and resolution behavior decide.

## Immediate implementation sequence

1. **Completed/rejected:** separate raw-leader and scale-law payload kernels
   plus ordered incidence role in one scalar complete algebra. It preserves
   astronaut and Pikachu but reverses the decisive coffee relation.
2. Define a vector-valued packet ABI which preserves the coordinate tuple
   through transport, comparison, and alpha recomposition; no scalar collapse
   is permitted before the named relational audit.
3. Render all-anchor soft recompositions for the five frozen controls; retain
   landmark anchors only as a readable subset of that exhaustive output.
4. Add packet self-return and full spectral-projector diagnostics separately
   for each coordinate and for their joint operator-valued representation.
5. Run the complete 128/256/384 and corrected-hard matrix before modifying the
   operator.
6. If the typed packet passes, investigate exact central/projective
   idempotents of the generated typed algebra. If it fails, localize the failed
   coordinate with the same nulls rather than adding a learned or hand-tuned
   merge rule.
