What the model keeps.

Pending obligations carry collision deadlines and motion revisions. Standing obligations carry contact witnesses and support/friction forces. Separating planes certify intervals with no collision. Equilibrium requires a reconstructible force and torque balance; low velocity alone does not qualify.

Contact support uses a finite horizon, orientation uses a numerical integrator, and position correction is a separate approximation. The study records failures, unconverged updates, and removed equilibrium energy. Finite speed tests do not establish correctness for arbitrary geometry or speed.
