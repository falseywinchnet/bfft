Contributing captures

These are local contrast measurements on common image support. The laptop’s upper and lower regions constrain each fit separately. A candidate must pass model selection and a final holdout check; an unresolved view keeps its prior map.

The complete scene

Both versions use native CONV sampling, ordinary linear-light averaging, and fixed exposure gains. The existing laptop region and reference capture are experimental inputs; correspondence parameters are estimated from the images. The difficult partial view contains blur in the original capture and a cropped left edge. Intrinsic blur is distinct from registration error.
