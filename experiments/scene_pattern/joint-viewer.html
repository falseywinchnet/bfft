Testing the distortion basis jointly

The shade and shaft constrain one local mapping per capture. The search compares affine, projective, and quadratic models, balances the two regions, and checks each region before accepting a correction. Parameters come from image evidence.

Accepted models and measured residuals

Residuals are local contrast disagreement on common support, not ground-truth geometric error. The lower region includes image overlap that need not depict the same visible surface in every capture. Model selection and the final holdout gate use separate blocks. Holdout failure retains the prior map; these reported scores are acceptance evidence, not an independent post-acceptance test.

What changed in the method

Missing-image boundaries use normalized convolution. Region-balanced fitting prevents the larger shade region from overwhelming the shaft. Each candidate is checked through direct sampling of the original capture, avoiding the extra blur from resampling a previously warped image. Outside observed fit support, a converged screened harmonic continuation carries the measured displacements outward. Positive sampled Jacobians are checked after composition with the previous fields.

The shade/shaft audit regions and reference photo remain manually selected. This is a reusable model search tested on that controlled region, not automatic surface discovery or a complete scene reconstruction. Unresolved observations remain represented in the average.
