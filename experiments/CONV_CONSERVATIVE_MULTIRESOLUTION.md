# Conservative scale coordinates for CONV

## 1. Discretization and one-dimensional split

At the multiresolution interface, a raster entry is a cell average.  For an
even fine line (x=(x_0,\ldots,x_{2N-1})), define

\[
c_i=\frac{x_{2i}+x_{2i+1}}2,
\qquad
q_i^\star=\frac{x_{2i+1}-x_{2i}}2.
\tag{1}
\]

Thus (c_i) is the zeroth centered child moment and (q_i^\star) is the
first.  Let (p=\mathcal J(c)/4) be the compact CONV first-jet proposal.  The
factor (1/4) is fixed by geometry: the two child centers are displaced by
(\pm1/4) of the parent spacing.  Admission gives

\[
\widehat q=\mathcal A_c p,
\qquad d=q^\star-\widehat q.
\tag{2}
\]

The inverse is

\[
x_{2i}=c_i-\widehat q_i-d_i,
\qquad
x_{2i+1}=c_i+\widehat q_i+d_i.
\tag{3}
\]

The interpolation manifold is the zero-detail section (d=0).  The detail
is not the child variation itself; it is the child variation not accounted
for by transported coarse variation.

## 2. The one-dimensional admissible polytope

Put

\[
\delta_i=c_{i+1}-c_i.
\]

Delete-zero sign order is preserved when zero secants are filled from their
nearest witnessed lineage.  Denote the resulting interval signs by
(s_i\in\{-1,1\}).  The moment in cell (i) is assigned a sign (t_i):

\[
t_0=s_0,
\qquad t_{N-1}=s_{N-2},
\]

and for (0<i<N-1),

\[
t_i=
\begin{cases}
s_{i-1},&s_{i-1}=s_i,\\
s_{i-1}\text{ or }s_i,&s_{i-1}\ne s_i.
\end{cases}
\tag{4}
\]

At a transition, the sign having the least disagreement with (p_i) is
used.  This is the discrete location of the already witnessed extremum; it
does not introduce another sign lineage.

The fine currents generated by (q) are

\[
g_{2i}=2q_i,
\qquad
g_{2i+1}=\delta_i-q_i-q_{i+1}.
\tag{5}
\]

For a fixed transition assignment (t), the complete admissible set is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P(c,t)=
\left\{q:
t_iq_i\ge0,
\quad
s_i(\delta_i-q_i-q_{i+1})\ge0
\right\}.}
\tag{6}
\]

It is a nonempty closed convex polytope: (q=0) is feasible, and every
(q_i) is bounded by an incident secant belonging to the lineage to which
cell (i) was assigned.  Formula (6) includes monotone runs, extrema, and
all sign-change transitions.  For example, on an increasing run it reduces
to

\[
q_i\ge0,
\qquad q_i+q_{i+1}\le\delta_i.
\tag{7}
\]

The reference admission has no tuned limiter.  It first takes the orthant
part

\[
p_i^+=t_i\max(t_ip_i,0),
\]

then, independently on each sign lineage, retains the largest
(\alpha\in[0,1]) for which (\alpha p^+\in\mathcal P(c,t)).  Each
constraint is affine in (\alpha), so the result is a finite minimum of
explicit ratios.  Oppositely oriented moments at a transition relax, rather
than consume, the adjacent lineage capacity.

## 3. One-dimensional theorems

### Theorem 1: exact reconstruction

For every finite input admitted by (1)--(3), analysis followed by synthesis
is the identity in exact arithmetic.

**Proof.** Analysis stores (d_i=q_i^\star-\widehat q_i).  Substitution in
(3) gives (c_i\mp q_i^\star=x_{2i},x_{2i+1}).  No property of the predictor
or admission is required.  QED.

### Theorem 2: mass conservation and matched closure

Let (R) denote (1) and (S) denote (3).  Then

\[
R S(c,d)=c
\tag{8}
\]

for every detail, including (d=0).

**Proof.** The two synthesized children sum to (2c_i).  QED.

### Theorem 3: exact Nyquist rejection

The restriction transfer function is

\[
H(\omega)=\frac{1+e^{-i\omega}}2
=e^{-i\omega/2}\cos(\omega/2),
\tag{9}
\]

so (H(\pi)=0).  An alternating fine sequence therefore restricts to zero
for either amplitude and phase sign.

### Theorem 4: affine exactness

For (c_i=a+bi), the compact jet gives (p_i=b/4).  Formula (7) is strict
because (p_i+p_{i+1}=b/2\le b) when (b>0), with the reversed statement
when (b<0).  Hence

\[
S(c,0)_{2i}=a+b(i-1/4),
\quad
S(c,0)_{2i+1}=a+b(i+1/4),
\tag{10}
\]

which are the exact child-cell averages of an affine field.

### Theorem 5: ordered variation does not increase

After exact zeros are deleted, the fine-current signs are a subsequence of

\[
t_0,s_0,t_1,s_1,\ldots,s_{N-2},t_{N-1}.
\tag{11}
\]

Inside a coarse sign run, all entries in (11) have the same sign.  At a
coarse transition, (t_i) equals one of its two adjacent signs, so the three
entries (s_{i-1},t_i,s_i) contain exactly one transition.  Consequently

\[
\operatorname{var}_{\pm}(D S(c,0))
\le
\operatorname{var}_{\pm}(Dc),
\tag{12}
\]

where (\operatorname{var}_{\pm}) counts changes in the nonzero sign
sequence.  This is the relevant ringing invariant.  It is not a claim that
the total amplitude variation or the range of cell averages must contract.

The latter would contradict affine exactness at a finite boundary: a parent
average at the first cell center lies strictly inside the affine values of
its two children.  Stability must therefore be stated for the transform and
the ordered-current topology, not as a false coarse-range maximum principle.

### Theorem 6: finite-level stability

Restriction is an (\ell_\infty) contraction.  The polytope bounds every
admitted moment by an incident coarse secant; hence

\[
\|\widehat q\|_\infty\le2\|c\|_\infty,
\qquad
\|S(c,0)\|_\infty\le3\|c\|_\infty.
\tag{13}
\]

For two detail states on the same coarse state,

\[
\|S(c,d)-S(c,d')\|_\infty=\|d-d'\|_\infty.
\tag{14}
\]

Thus a (J)-level synthesis obeys the explicit lattice-cardinality-independent
bound

\[
\|x\|_\infty
\le3^J\|c^{(J)}\|_\infty
+\sum_{j=1}^{J}3^{j-1}\|d^{(j)}\|_\infty,
\tag{15}
\]

with exact inverse and with (12) holding inductively on every discarded-detail
level.  The coefficient depends on the declared number of levels, not on the
number of cells or queried samples.

## 4. Two-dimensional moment form

For a fine (2\times2) block ordered as (x_{00},x_{01},x_{10},x_{11}), set

\[
\begin{aligned}
c&=\tfrac14(x_{00}+x_{01}+x_{10}+x_{11}),\\
q_x&=\tfrac14(-x_{00}+x_{01}-x_{10}+x_{11}),\\
q_y&=\tfrac14(-x_{00}-x_{01}+x_{10}+x_{11}),\\
q_{xy}&=\tfrac14(x_{00}-x_{01}-x_{10}+x_{11}).
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]

The exact inverse is

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
x_{00}=c-q_x-q_y+q_{xy},&x_{01}=c+q_x-q_y-q_{xy},\\
x_{10}=c-q_x+q_y-q_{xy},&x_{11}=c+q_x+q_y+q_{xy}.
\end{array}
\tag{17}
\]

The mean is the scale-persistent mass.  The other three coefficients are the
horizontal, vertical, and mixed centered child moments.  CONV proposes

\[
p_x=\frac{\partial_x c}{4},
\qquad
p_y=\frac{\partial_y c}{4},
\qquad
p_{xy}=\frac{\partial_{xy}c}{16},
\tag{18}
\]

using the same compact two-jet closures as interpolation.

## 5. Two-dimensional face-current polytope

Inside one block, the horizontal currents are

\[
2(q_x-q_{xy}),\qquad2(q_x+q_{xy}),
\tag{19}
\]

and the vertical currents are

\[
2(q_y-q_{xy}),\qquad2(q_y+q_{xy}).
\tag{20}
\]

For horizontally adjacent blocks (A,B), with
(\delta_x=c_B-c_A), the upper and lower cross-face currents are

\[
\begin{aligned}
h^-_{AB}&=\delta_x-q_{x,A}-q_{x,B}
          +q_{y,A}-q_{y,B}+q_{xy,A}+q_{xy,B},\\
h^+_{AB}&=\delta_x-q_{x,A}-q_{x,B}
          -q_{y,A}+q_{y,B}-q_{xy,A}-q_{xy,B}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{21}
\]

For vertically adjacent blocks (A,C), with
(\delta_y=c_C-c_A), the left and right cross-face currents are

\[
\begin{aligned}
v^-_{AC}&=\delta_y+q_{x,A}-q_{x,C}
          -q_{y,A}-q_{y,C}+q_{xy,A}+q_{xy,C},\\
v^+_{AC}&=\delta_y-q_{x,A}+q_{x,C}
          -q_{y,A}-q_{y,C}-q_{xy,A}-q_{xy,C}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{22}
\]

Let (t_x,t_y) be the block signs assigned by the one-dimensional lineage
rule on each coarse row and column, and let (s_x,s_y) be the corresponding
coarse face signs.  The two-dimensional admissible face-current set is the
finite intersection

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
t_x(q_x\pm q_{xy})&\ge0,&
s_xh^\pm&\ge0,\\
t_y(q_y\pm q_{xy})&\ge0,&
s_yv^\pm&\ge0.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{23}
\]

Every inequality is affine in the moment field.  Thus (23) is a closed convex
face-current polyhedron containing the zero moment field.  The reference
operator first admits the proposal to the internal cone

\[
t_xq_x\ge|q_{xy}|,
\qquad t_yq_y\ge|q_{xy}|,
\tag{24}
\]

then retains the largest common (\alpha\in[0,1]) for which the ray
(\alpha(q_x,q_y,q_{xy})) satisfies every cross-face inequality in (23).
As in one dimension, this is a finite minimum of affine intersection ratios,
not an iterative fit.

For each pair of fine rows, the horizontal sign sequence interleaves
(t_x,s_x,t_x,s_x,\ldots); for each pair of fine columns, the vertical sign
sequence interleaves (t_y,s_y,t_y,s_y,\ldots).  The proof of (12) therefore
applies factorwise.  Equations (16)--(17) prove exact reconstruction after the
three residual moment fields are restored, and their sum proves exact 2x2
mass conservation.

The two-dimensional restriction response is

\[
H(\omega_x,\omega_y)=
e^{-i(\omega_x+\omega_y)/2}
\cos(\omega_x/2)\cos(\omega_y/2),
\tag{25}
\]

which annihilates both axial Nyquist lines and the checkerboard corner.

## 6. Verification boundary

The executable reference and theorem tests are
`conv_conservative_multiresolution.py` and
`test_conv_conservative_multiresolution.py`.  The current tests cover exact
analysis/synthesis identities to binary64 roundoff, pair and block mass,
Nyquist sequences, affine fields, randomized sign-transition lines,
multilevel inversion, the 2x2 moment inverse, and two-dimensional factorwise
face-current certificates.  Fidelity experiments are deliberately separate;
they do not establish any theorem above.
