# Direct joint-CONV warps

This experiment defines and tests one direct inverse-warp operator for
rotation, skew, anisotropic affine maps, and homographies. It does not use
three-shear decomposition or any intermediate raster.

## Canonical operator

For source samples `Y` and one target-to-source map `F`, source analysis
constructs a shared bidegree-(5,5) Bernstein atlas `U_Y`. Point synthesis is

\[
  W_FY(q)=U_Y(F(q)).
\]

The atlas is produced by one fixed sequence:

1. Native CONV factor passes evaluate the complete two-order proposal on the
   global fifth-step lattice.
2. The fixed inverse of the six-node Bernstein collocation matrix forms the
   bivariate proposal patches.
3. Every shared control is clipped to the intersection of the channelwise
   min--max ranges of its incident declared two-jet supports.
4. For each cell, the cone generated by the `Q1` corner currents in that same
   support is converted to its finite planar dual.
5. Shared-edge and cell-interior corrections from the degree-elevated `Q1`
   baseline are admitted by exact scalar margin capacities, followed by one
   maximal common ray step.

The public constructor is `finite_joint_control_nets`. It has no witness,
range, solver, or iteration option. Older projectors and rejected witnesses in
`joint_reference.py` are derivation records only.

For a vector-valued raster, the admissible set is the direct product of the
channelwise current cones and support intervals. All channels use one atlas
topology and the same inverse map. The proof is componentwise and does not
claim invariance under an arbitrary change of channel basis.

## Analytic properties

The current implementation and tests cover the following properties.

- **Cardinality:** source vertices are fixed exactly.
- **Integrability:** adjacent patches share one complete control edge; the
  current is the gradient of one continuous piecewise-polynomial potential.
- **Affine reproduction:** degree-elevated `Q1` is feasible, and affine data
  pass the complete construction.
- **Support range:** every Bernstein control lies in every incident declared
  support interval. Bernstein convexity extends this bound to the complete
  patch.
- **Joint current admission:** `grad(U)` lies in the witnessed support-current
  cone wherever that cone is proper. Equivalently, every dual directional
  derivative is nonnegative over the complete cell.
- **Coordinate exchange:** the real-arithmetic construction is axis
  equivariant. The native FP32 compilation differs under exchanged evaluation
  order by at most `4.2e-7` in the current test.
- **Warp transport:** along a target curve `gamma`,

  \[
    \frac{d}{ds}U(F(\gamma(s)))=
    (J_F(\gamma(s))\gamma'(s))^T\nabla U.
  \]

  Thus the source cone theorem transports through affine and projective maps
  without selecting a new target-space owner or orientation.

## Warped pixels

The raster-reduction functional is

\[
  B_FY[j]=|P_j|^{-1}\int_{P_j}U_Y(F(q))\,dq,
\]

where the endpoint-aligned target Voronoi basins `P_j` partition the target
domain.

For affine maps, each mapped basin is split at source knots. Positive fan
triangulation and order-six Duffy--Gauss quadrature integrate every resulting
bidegree-(5,5) patch exactly. A focused conservation test equates the weighted
target sum with the independently integrated source atlas to `3e-12`.

For a homography, source-coordinate area density is

\[
  |\det D F^{-1}(p)|=|\det H^{-1}|/|\ell(p)|^3.
\]

The implementation expands `(1+z)^-3` through order six, integrates the
polynomial part exactly, and bounds the remaining binomial tail in closed
form. Triangle subdivision occurs only when this certificate exceeds the
requested error. The exact integral above remains the operator definition;
the returned numerical result carries its computed error bound.

## Reproduction

Run the complete focused suite:

```sh
python3 -m unittest \
  experiments.conv_warp.test_joint_reference \
  experiments.conv_warp.test_conv_warp
```

Generate the band-admitted smooth census:

```sh
python3 -m experiments.conv_warp.run_canonical_warp_study \
  --source-side 25 --target-side 17 \
  --out output/support_geometry/conv_warp_canonical_range_25_17.json
```

Generate the exact polygon affine/projective census:

```sh
python3 -m experiments.conv_warp.run_projective_study \
  --source-side 17 --target-side 13 \
  --out output/support_geometry/conv_warp_projective_range_compiled_17_13.json
```

Generate the curved current census at sizes 9, 13, 17, and 25 with
`run_curved_probe --witness finite_support_gradient_cone`, and generate the
straight-interface census with `run_interface_probe`.

Render the two evidence figures:

```sh
python3 -m experiments.conv_warp.render_canonical_metrics \
  --out output/support_geometry/conv_warp_canonical_metrics.png

python3 -m experiments.conv_warp.render_projective_plate \
  --source-side 17 --target-side 13 \
  --out output/support_geometry/conv_warp_projective_plate_canonical.png
```

## Current measured evidence

All values below come from the JSON artifacts named above.

- On the 16-case sharp affine/projective corpus, joint CONV has the lowest
  exact-truth MSE in 7 point cases and 7 warped-pixel cases. Its mean MSE is
  `2.10019e-2` for points and `7.37702e-3` for warped pixels. Measured range
  excursion is zero to floating roundoff in every case.
- Factor CONV reaches point/basin excursions of `0.229/0.143`; Lanczos-3
  reaches `0.282/0.199`.
- At curved-interface source sides 13, 17, and 25, mean and maximum joint-CONV
  wrong-way normal variation and range excursion are at floating roundoff. At
  side 9, the range law remains exact and mean wrong-way variation is
  `1.94e-3`; the six-node support spans competing conic normals at that scale.
- On 18 straight interfaces at source sides 17 and 33, joint CONV has zero
  sign changes and floating-zero wrong-way variation/range excursion. Factor
  CONV is likewise exact there; the joint law is specifically needed for
  curved supports. Lanczos-3 has 7--9 sign changes and about `0.277` mean
  wrong-way variation.
- On the band-admitted Fourier corpus, the support maximum principle raises
  mean point MSE from `1.06820e-4` for factor CONV to `1.48764e-4` for joint
  CONV. Lanczos-3 is lower at `4.57424e-5`; bilinear is
  `1.22893e-3`. This is the measured fidelity cost of forbidding extrema not
  present in the declared sample support, not evidence of a numerical error.
- A nonconstant projective plane integral differs from an independent
  40-point tensor calculation by `1.18e-11`, inside its `5.84e-10`
  certificate. In the sharp projective corpus the largest returned
  certificate is `1.52e-11`.

These experiments prove neither recovery of unsampled detail nor universal
MSE dominance. The proved statements concern cardinality, integrability,
support range, admitted current cones, affine polynomial integration, and the
reported projective error enclosure.
