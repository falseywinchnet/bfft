# CMU ARCTIC calibration provenance

The calibration lane uses CMU ARCTIC waveforms and the corresponding `.lab`
phone timings from the official FestVox distribution:

- corpus overview: <http://www.festvox.org/cmu_arctic/>
- BDL speaker: <http://www.festvox.org/cmu_arctic/dbs_bdl.html>
- corpus license: <http://www.festvox.org/cmu_arctic/cmu_arctic/cmu_us_bdl_arctic/COPYING>

The corpus describes roughly 1,150 phonetically balanced utterances per
speaker. Its phonetic labels were generated by the CMU Sphinx/FestVox toolchain
and were not hand-corrected. Results must therefore call these labels
independent timed evidence, not a human-verified oracle.

The exploratory same-sentence calibration used these five utterances for BDL,
SLT, and CLB:

```text
arctic_a0108 arctic_a0019 arctic_a0117 arctic_a0041 arctic_a0095
```

Those five utterances cover the declared 39-phone stress-stripped ARPAbet
inventory, but using the same sentences across speakers introduces lexical
context as a confound. The authoritative unseen-sentence CLB holdout instead
uses:

```text
arctic_a0027 arctic_a0058 arctic_a0099
```

These are disjoint from the BDL+SLT calibration sentences and contain 116
timed phone crops spanning 38 of the 39 phone types; `ZH` is absent. The
multiscale/Gabor weights were frozen before this holdout was run.

The relative-duration coefficient `0.08` was selected by maximizing mean
top-five recall over the BDL→SLT and SLT→BDL calibration folds. It was then
held fixed for the disjoint CLB evaluation. The three reported disjoint
geometries are raw raster (43.10% top-five), multiscale/Gabor (46.55%), and
multiscale/Gabor plus relative duration (55.17%).

After CLB had been inspected, speaker RMS became an untouched confirmation
speaker on the same three disjoint utterances. The fixed raw,
multiscale/Gabor, and duration-coupled top-five results were 38.79%, 47.41%,
and 54.31%. Activity-morphology parameters developed on CLB were likewise
frozen before the RMS segmentation audit.
